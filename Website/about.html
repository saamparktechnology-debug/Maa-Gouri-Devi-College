About Us

about the college

Built by the Gouri Devi Trust to train teachers close to home.

Maa Gouri Devi College of Education has run its B.Ed and D.El.Ed programmes from the Dasagram campus since 2015, giving students of Sabang and the surrounding blocks a recognised teacher training option without leaving the district.

Our story

A trust-run college with a single, local purpose.

The Gouri Devi Trust established the college in 2015 to answer a specific, practical need: the district's schools needed trained teachers, and its students needed an accessible place to train. The Dasagram campus was built with that in mind — classrooms, a library, hostel blocks, and open ground under one roof, all reachable within the sub-division.

Today the college runs two NCTE-approved programmes: B.Ed for upper primary and secondary teaching, and D.El.Ed for primary teaching, each following its respective affiliating body's curriculum.

Our Programs

B.Ed & D.El.Ed

Bachelor of Education

B.Ed | Upper Primary & Secondary

Two-year programme approved by NCTE and affiliated to BSAEU. Focus on pedagogy, educational psychology, and school-based practice.

Diploma in Elementary Education

D.El.Ed | Primary Level

Two-year diploma recognised by NCTE and affiliated to WB Board of Primary Education. Emphasis on child development, language, and EVS methods.

School Internship

Practice Teaching

Structured internship in nearby schools, supervised by faculty. Trainees plan lessons, teach, and reflect on real classroom experiences.

Continuous Assessment

Internal & External Evaluation

Regular internal assessments, assignments, and semester-end exams. Emphasis on continuous learning and feedback throughout the course.
