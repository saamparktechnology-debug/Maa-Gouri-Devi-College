Our Courses

Programmes on offer

Two NCTE-approved routes into teaching.

Both programmes are run from the Dasagram campus, with classroom instruction, school-based practice, and library access included.

B.Ed — Bachelor of Education

Affiliated to BSAEU

The B.Ed programme trains graduates to teach at the upper primary and secondary level. It combines the theory of teaching — educational psychology, pedagogy, and school management — with subject-specific teaching method and supervised practice in schools.

  • Foundations of education and educational psychology
  • Pedagogy of school subjects
  • Supervised teaching practice in schools
  • Assessment for learning and classroom management

D.El.Ed — Diploma in Elementary Education

Affiliated to WB Board of Primary Education

The D.El.Ed programme trains teachers for the primary classroom. Coursework covers child development, primary-level pedagogy across subjects, and hands-on teaching practice, following the syllabus set by the West Bengal Board of Primary Education.

  • Child development and primary pedagogy
  • Language, mathematics and environmental studies teaching methods
  • Practical teaching in primary schools
  • Classroom-based assessment techniques
